Botany

About Department

The Botany Department was established in 1963, and postgraduate courses were introduced in 1987. The department received recognition for admitting students for M.Sc. (By Research) and Ph.D. in 1968 and 1972 respectively. The University of Mumbai also had M. Phil program for four years, and students were registered for the same. Today, as one of the University Departments in the discipline of Botany, the Department of Botany at Ramniranjan Jhunjhunwala College enjoys a position second to none in Mumbai as well as Maharashtra.

Infrastructure

There is a separate Library for Botany. The students have access to both the Central Library and the Departmental Library. The departmental library has more than 600 books. Updating is done on the recommendation of the faculty members as well as the Library Committee. The Library also subscribes to periodicals and magazines.
