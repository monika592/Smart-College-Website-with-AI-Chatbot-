Banking and Insurance (B.B.I)

About Department

The department was established in 2005–06. It aims to provide students with a strong foundation in banking, finance, and insurance, equipping them with practical skills and theoretical knowledge to meet industry standards.

Infrastructure

Students have access to the Central Library. Updating is done on the recommendation of the faculty members as well as the Library Committee. The Library also subscribes to periodicals and magazines.

Admissions

A candidate for being eligible for admission to the Bachelor of Banking and Insurance (B.B.I) course must have passed the Higher Secondary School Certificate Examination (Std. XII) conducted by the Maharashtra State Board of Secondary and Higher Secondary Education or its equivalent.

Candidates who have passed a Diploma in any branch of Engineering or any equivalent Government-recognized qualification are also eligible. Admission is granted on a merit basis as per the norms of the University of Mumbai.
