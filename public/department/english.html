About Department

The Department of English was established in 1963, the same year the college was founded. The department offers both undergraduate and postgraduate programs in English Language and Literature. It aims to develop students’ literary sensibility, communication skills, and cultural awareness through an interdisciplinary approach.

The department organizes seminars, elocution competitions, literary festivals, and guest lectures to promote critical appreciation and creative writing skills among students.

Infrastructure

The Department is equipped with a digital classroom, a departmental library with reference books, and access to audio-visual learning tools. Students can also access the central library for reference and research purposes.

Admissions

A candidate for being eligible for admission to the three-year integrated course leading to the degree of Bachelor of Arts (B.A.) in English must have passed the Higher Secondary School Certificate Examination (Std. XII) in any stream or equivalent from a recognized board.
