Economics

About Department

The Department of Economics was established in 1963. It offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs that develop analytical and critical thinking skills among students. The department focuses on areas like microeconomics, macroeconomics, development studies, and public finance. It promotes research and organizes guest lectures, seminars, and workshops regularly.

Admissions

A candidate for being eligible for admission to the three-year integrated course leading to the degree of Bachelor of Arts (B.A.) must have passed Higher Secondary School Certificate Examination (Std. XII) in any stream or its equivalent from a recognized board. Admission is based on merit.
