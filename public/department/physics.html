Physics

About Department

The Department of Physics was established in 1964. It runs undergraduate three-year Bachelor of Science program in Physics and postgraduate Master of Science in Physics course. The department has 09 teaching staff and 14 non-teaching staff. Around 800 students are enrolled in F.Y.B.Sc. to M.Sc. programs.

The department is a part of prestigious DBT STAR college grant that recognizes need for nurturing the basic science culture and to reach its grants’ goal of the education system–Thru students. Under this scheme, the department conducts various subject-related events and workshops to inculcate various qualities like logical reasoning, experimental skills, scientific knowledge and research skills in students. In recognition of this work, the DBT has granted us the STAR status.

To succeed in any discipline, one needs to have broad-based knowledge in science and a deep knowledge of specific research area. To cater to these needs, Physics Department started M.Sc. in 2016–17. It has two research laboratories recognized by the University of Mumbai for M.Sc. and Ph.D. students. Students are guided by highly qualified and experienced teachers.

The faculties are awarded minor and major research projects funded by UGC, CSIR, SERB, ISRO and Mumbai University. Starting from 2018–19, the college became autonomous allowing the department to experiment with novel approaches to teaching and evaluation.

With the autonomy, we started conducting M.Sc. lectures with help of distinguished visiting faculty from 2018–19. Students carry out their M.Sc. projects in institutes like BARC, HBCSE, ICT, IITB, SAMEER, TIFR, etc. From 2019–20, we also introduced new electives in Material Science and restructured the courses to include Computational Physics.

Infrastructure

There is a separate Library for Physics. Students have access to both the Central Library and the Departmental Library. The departmental library has more than 600 books. Updating is done based on the recommendation of the faculty members and Library Committee. The Library also subscribes to periodicals and magazines.

Admissions

B.Sc. Physics / M.Sc. Physics: Candidates seeking admission must have passed Higher Secondary School Certificate Examination (Std. XII) in Science conducted by Maharashtra State Board of Secondary and Higher Secondary Education or its equivalent in Science stream. Admission will be on merit.
