Computer Science

About Department

The Computer Science Department was established in 1999. The B.Sc. (Computer Science) program with an intake of 60 students was introduced the same year, and the M.Sc. (Computer Science) program with an intake of 40 students was started in 2001.

Infrastructure

The department has a well-equipped computer lab, access to a departmental library, and the Central Library. The departmental library houses over 270 books and subscribes to relevant journals and magazines. All computers are connected through a high-speed internet facility.

Admissions

B.Sc. Computer Science: Admission is based on merit. Candidates must have passed H.S.C. (12th Science) with Mathematics and Statistics as subjects, or equivalent qualification.

Eligibility: Aggregate marks in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics are considered. Diploma holders in related fields may also apply as per rules.
