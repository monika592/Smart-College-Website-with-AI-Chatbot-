Sociology

About Department

The Department of Sociology was established in 1963. It offers undergraduate courses that provide a deep understanding of human behavior, social institutions, culture, and societal development.

The department aims to create awareness about social issues and encourage analytical and critical thinking among students through seminars, workshops, and field studies. It also promotes research-oriented learning through small projects and assignments.

Infrastructure

The Department of Sociology has access to the Central Library, digital resources, and research databases. It also maintains case study materials and journals related to contemporary social issues.

Admissions

A candidate for being eligible for admission to the three-year integrated course leading to the degree of Bachelor of Arts (B.A.) in Sociology must have passed the Higher Secondary School Certificate Examination (Std. XII) in any stream or equivalent from a recognized board.
