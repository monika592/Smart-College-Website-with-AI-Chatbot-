Information Technology

About Department

The Department of Information Technology at Ramniranjan Jhunjhunwala College, Ghatkopar, Mumbai was established in 2001. The department offers both undergraduate (B.Sc. I.T.) and postgraduate (M.Sc. I.T.) programmes. The M.Sc. I.T. programme was introduced in 2016.

Infrastructure

The department provides access to both the Central Library and a Departmental Library. The departmental library houses over 1200 books and subscribes to academic journals and magazines. Students have access to a well-equipped computer lab, reliable internet connectivity, and modern instruments for IT and related courses.

Admissions
B.Sc. I.T.

B.Sc. I.T. is a three-year full-time course designed to provide the latest skills and practices in the IT industry.

Eligibility: Students who have passed the 12th standard with Mathematics as one of the subjects, or those with a Diploma in Computer Science or allied fields, are eligible for admission.

Curriculum: Covers Programming, Networking, Database Management, Data Analysis, Embedded Systems, IoT, and Information Security.

Career Prospects: Graduates can pursue M.Sc. IT, MCA, MBA, or careers in Software Development, Networking, Cybersecurity, Data Analytics, or IT Consulting.
