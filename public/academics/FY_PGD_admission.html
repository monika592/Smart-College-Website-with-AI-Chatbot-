Important Instructions

STEP 1: Filling of the Online Admission Form

  • R.J. College Students who have passed TY in 2021–22, 2022–23, 2023–24, or 2024–25 can use their TY login ID and password.
  • R.J. students before 2021 or from other colleges must sign up fresh on the Admission Portal.
  • Enter valid email ID and mobile number (WhatsApp) for communication.
  • Fill all details correctly and select Hindi Linguistic Minority if applicable.
  • Provisional merit list will be displayed before counselling. Admission is based on Sem V SGPA.

STEP 2: Counselling Session for Admission

  • All applicants must attend the counselling session. Attendance is mandatory.
  • Seats will be allotted as per merit and attendance during counselling.

STEP 3: Admission

  • Students allotted seats must confirm admission within the stipulated time. Vacant seats will be reallotted on merit.
  • All admissions are provisional and subject to document verification.
  • Incorrect details (SGPA, category, etc.) will lead to cancellation.
  • No donations accepted for admission. Principal’s decision is final.
Note: If payment is not reflected, wait 48 hours. If refunded, repay through the portal.

Documents Required

  • Passport-size photograph
  • Signature (digital)
  • Aadhaar Card
  • Caste / Reservation Certificate (if applicable)
  • Marksheet copies (Sem I–V)
  • University Enrollment form (not required currently)
