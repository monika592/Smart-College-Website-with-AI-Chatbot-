Minority Status

The College has linguistic minority status. 50% of the seats are reserved for students belonging to the Hindi-speaking community as per merit. For the remaining seats, reservation policy will be as per the directives of the University of Mumbai.

Degree College

Students who have passed the Higher Secondary Certificate (H.S.C) Examination conducted by the Maharashtra State Board of Secondary & Higher Secondary Education, Pune are eligible for admission to the F.Y.B.A./B.Sc./B.Com./BMS. (Subject to fulfilling other conditions).

Documents & Certificates

A. Documents issued by the college office
  1. Bonafide Certificate: Students admitted to Junior or Degree College are eligible to get a Bonafide Certificate on application and payment of Rs.10/- (Rupees Ten only).
  2. Railway Concession: Issued to students below 25 years for season tickets nearest to home station.
  3. Transcript: Issued on request for students going abroad with complete academic details.
  4. Transfer Certificate: Issued for students leaving one college to join another after required attendance verification.
  5. Recommendation Letter: Issued for students applying for postgraduate or foreign universities upon request.
  6. Migration Certificate: Issued for students leaving the University of Mumbai jurisdiction for higher education elsewhere.
  7. College Leaving Certificate: Issued to students who have passed the XIIth Standard Examination.
B. Documents to be submitted by the student
  1. Provisional Eligibility: For students from other Universities or Boards to get eligibility from the University of Mumbai.
  2. Original Mark Sheet
  3. Original Passing Certificate
  4. Migration Certificate
  5. Caste Certificate for Validation: Applicable for students belonging to SC/ST/OBC categories, requiring attested copies for verification.
  6. Migration Certificate: Applicable for students transferring from other Universities for higher studies.
