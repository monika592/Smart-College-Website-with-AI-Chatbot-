Best Practices

Objective: To upgrade knowledge and technical literacy of library and office staff.
Context: Many support staff need regular training to handle digital library software and e-resources.
The Practice: Workshops and practical sessions conducted annually focusing on Koha, DSpace, and online databases.
Evidence of Success: Improved efficiency and user satisfaction; faster cataloguing and issue process.
Resources Required: ICT lab, library hall, computers, and external experts.

Objective: To encourage self-learning and innovation among students.
Context: Students often need extra time beyond class hours for project work and experiments.
The Practice: Extended hours for library and laboratories till 7 PM; teacher mentorship during open hours.
Evidence of Success: Notable improvement in research projects and innovation awards.
Resources Required: Staff support, electricity, and maintenance resources.

Objective: To promote a culture of continuous professional growth among faculty.
The Practice: “Each One Teach One” sessions where senior professors mentor junior faculty in ICT tools, research writing, and pedagogy.
Evidence of Success: Faculty members published more papers and attended national FDPs.
Resources Required: Smart classroom, projector, internet access.

Objective: To bridge theory and real-world problem solving.
The Practice: Students work on community or industry-linked projects evaluated by a panel.
Evidence of Success: Many students received internships and start-up mentorship offers.
Resources Required: Laboratories, software tools, and mentor support.

Objective: To foster inclusivity and social empathy among students.
The Practice: Regular outreach visits to NGOs, orphanages, and senior citizen homes.
Evidence of Success: Students developed empathy and social awareness; improved NSS participation.
Resources Required: Transportation, coordination with NGOs, and faculty involvement.

Objective: To prepare students for employability and entrepreneurship.
The Practice: Certification programs in Communication Skills, Cyber Security, Data Analytics, and Yoga.
Evidence of Success: Over 500 students completed at least one course annually.
Resources Required: Expert trainers, labs, and online platforms.

Objective: To ensure holistic student development.
The Practice: Mentorship programs, remedial teaching, career counseling, and alumni networking.
Evidence of Success: Improved retention rate and placement success.
Resources Required: Counseling cell, mentor logs, alumni portal.
