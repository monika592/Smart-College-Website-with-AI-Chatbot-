Program Eligibility

The eligibility criteria for admission to undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs at R.J. College are based on the University of Mumbai norms and college-specific requirements. Below is a summary of the required qualifications and subject combinations for various programs.

Documents Required for Admission

  • Original mark sheet of the qualifying examination
  • Passing Certificate
  • Transfer/Migration Certificate (if applicable)
  • Caste Certificate (if applicable)
  • Passport-size photographs
