Examinations



For FY Under NEP from A.Y. 2023–2024 Onwards:

  1. A learner appearing for first-year examination under NEP will have maximum of 22 credits and examinations will be of maximum 550 marks.
  2. Courses having 2 credits, 3 credits, and 4 credits will have examinations of 50, 75, 100 marks respectively.
  3. With regard to Co-curricular and Extra-curricular activities, continuous evaluation under autonomy shall continue for all courses.
  4. Minimum of 40% marks shall be required in both Internal and External to pass the examination.
  5. Appearance for SEE for every paper is compulsory irrespective of internal marks.
  6. The benefit of grace marks, condonation, and ordinances will be applicable to the candidate as per University guidelines.

Explanation:

Ordinance 5042A: The benefit of grace marks is applicable if the candidate passes the entire semester/year of examination.

Ordinance 5043A & 5044A: Grace marks for higher grade or class shall be applicable only if the student has cleared all subjects.

Ordinance 5045A: Condonation of deficiency in marks is given if a student is short by not more than 1% in the total aggregate.

Ordinance 5046A: Marks may be moderated where necessary by the moderation committee.

Ordinance 5048AAB: Results may be amended due to clerical, printing, or evaluation errors within 6 months from result declaration.

Ordinance 5049A: The examiner or controller may take disciplinary action against students attempting to use unfair means.

Ordinance 0.2298A: Students participating in NSS/NCC/Sports/LLLE are entitled to additional benefit marks.

The College follows the Credit Based Semester and Grading System (CBSGS) for Degree College students.

  1. The grade for a particular course will be based on combined performance in Internal Assessment and Semester End Examination.
  2. Passing Standard: Minimum of 40% in both internal and external separately.
  3. Additional examinations are conducted 20 days after declaration of result for failures due to valid reasons.
  4. The grade sheet of such learners will show “N.E.” if not eligible for the Semester End Examination.
  5. The learner shall earn the credits only after qualifying in both internal and external exams.
  6. Final Grade Point = weighted average of SGPA/CGPA as per University norms.

The University of Mumbai introduced the Credit Based Semester and Grading System (CBSGS) for Degree College students from A.Y. 2011–2012 onwards.

  • Each subject treated as an independent head.
  • Theory and practical examination: Minimum 40% required in both internal and external.
  • Supplementary examination available for valid reasons only.
  • Moderation, condonation, and grace marks applicable as per university norms.

Refer to detailed ordinance numbers 5042A–5049A and 0.229A for comprehensive examination regulations under autonomy.
