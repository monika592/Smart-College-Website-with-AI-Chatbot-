SCHOLARSHIP

A scholarship is a financial aid for a student to further their education. Scholarships are awarded based upon various criteria and are granted by College, Trusts and Governments. Below are details of all scholarships available to students.

Overview:

Education concession to the children of Ex-servicemen is given under No. MIS1465/H, dated 23/11/1965.

Eligibility (As per GR Dated 14.01.1985):
  • Students should be Son/Daughter/Wife/Widow of an Ex-Serviceman.
  • Applicable only for Government and aided colleges.
  • Only Maharashtra students can apply; those studying outside Maharashtra are not eligible.
Documents Required:
  • Eligibility Certificate from Collector and President of DSSA board.
  • Admission receipt.
  • Domicile certificate.

Overview:

Students admitted for Post Graduation who have secured 60% (Arts/Commerce/Law) or 70% (Science) are eligible.

  • Parental income ≤ ₹75,000 per annum.
  • Student should be a resident of Maharashtra and not working full-time/part-time.
  • Selection based on merit.
Documents Required:
  • Income Certificate (Tahsildar).
  • Previous year marksheet.
  • Domicile Certificate.

Overview:

Sanction set: 1208 students in Arts, Commerce, Science, Law graduation. Selection based on merit.

  • Fresh candidates must have 60% in 12th standard.
  • Applicable for Maharashtra residents only.
Documents Required:
  • Previous year marksheet.
  • Domicile certificate.
  • Bonafide certificate.

Overview:

Sanction Set: 100 (Maths 50, Physics 50). For Science students with Maths/Physics as main subject.

Eligibility:
  • Minimum 60% in 12th Science, and ≥60% in Maths/Physics.
  • Maharashtra domicile required.
  • Should not receive any other State/Central scholarship.
Documents Required:
  • Previous year marksheet.
  • Domicile certificate.
  • College bonafide certificate.

Overview:

Concession under GR Dated 23.11.1965 & 31.12.1990 for children/widows of Freedom Fighters.

  • Applicant must be domiciled in Maharashtra.
  • Students studying outside Maharashtra not eligible.
Documents Required:
  • Freedom Fighter Certificate.
  • Current year fee receipt.
  • Domicile & Bonafide certificate.

Overview:

Scheme for Maharashtrian students studying in JNU (as per GR SCH1082/151160 dated 07/05/1983).

  • Applicable for both UG & PG students from Maharashtra in JNU.
  • Only 1 student selected by JNU per year.
Documents Required:
  • Fee receipt.
  • Domicile certificate.
  • Previous year marksheet.

Overview:

For SC/OBC/VJNT/SBC/ST & Physically Disabled students of Junior/Degree colleges.

  • Income ≤ ₹2.5 lakh (SC/ST), ≤ ₹1 lakh (OBC/VJNT/SBC).
  • First two children eligible (OBC/VJNT/SBC).
  • 50% attendance required.
Documents Required:
  • Income, Domicile, Caste, LC, Ration Card, Aadhaar, Bank Passbook, Marksheet, Fee Receipt, etc.

Overview:

For SC/OBC/VJNT/SBC/ST and physically disabled students of Junior/Degree colleges with higher income brackets.

Eligibility:
  • Income more than ₹2.5 lakh (SC/ST) or ₹1 lakh (OBC/VJNT/SBC).
  • Maximum ₹6 lakh family income.
  • Maharashtra domicile and 50% attendance.
Documents Required:
  • All income, domicile, marksheet, LC, caste, Aadhaar, non-creamy layer certificates, etc.

Overview:

In collaboration with Seth Bhojraj Hassomal Charitable Trust to support meritorious unaided-course students.

Provides 50% fee scholarship for economically backward students.

About the Trust:

Established in 1969, SBHCT supports causes in education, healthcare & women empowerment across India.

Eligibility Criteria:
  • Minimum 60% in HSC (XII).
  • Family income ≤ ₹3 lakh per annum.
  • Applicant should not hold any other scholarship.
  • Open category students from unaided section only.
