Knowledge Resource Centre (Library)

Knowledge Resource Centre (Library) plays a very important role in academic life. The library provides access to a wide range of information resources that support the college’s teaching, learning, and research activities. It is a hub of knowledge dissemination and user services to help in lifelong learning.

Objectives

  • To develop reading habits among students and faculty.
  • To provide access to information sources in all forms.
  • To promote the optimum use of library resources.
  • To support teaching, learning, and research activities.

Library Services

  • Circulation services for books, periodicals, CDs, and e-resources.
  • Reference services and research assistance.
  • Inter-library loan and document delivery services.
  • Internet access for academic purposes.
  • Reading room for students and staff.

Library Operations

  • Acquisition, classification, and cataloguing of resources.
  • Stock verification and maintenance of records.
  • Automation of library activities through software systems.
  • Digitization of resources for remote access.

Library Rules

  • Every user must carry an identity card and library card.
  • Silence must be maintained inside the library.
  • Books should be handled carefully; damages must be reported.
  • Borrowed books must be returned on or before the due date.
  • Users must sign the visitor’s register on entry and exit.

Study Centre

The library also serves as a study centre providing reading and research facilities for students of other colleges during vacations and holidays. Timings: 9.00 am to 5.00 pm.

Study Room

Study rooms are available for group discussions and individual studies. Open from 9.30 am to 5.30 pm (Monday to Saturday).
