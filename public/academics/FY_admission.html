Online Admission Procedure

  • All students must complete University of Mumbai pre-enrollment (college code: 171).
  • After that, fill the R.J. College Online Admission Form.
  • R.J. students from 2021–22 to 2024–25 can log in using their HSC ID and password.
  • Other college or earlier batch students must register fresh on the portal.
  • Upload all required documents and submit clearly scanned copies.
In-house Students: Direct admission is available for FYBA, FYBCom, and FYBSc traditional programs only. Fees must be paid within the stipulated time after approval.

National Education Policy (NEP) 2020

The NEP 2020 brings a multidisciplinary, flexible, and skill-based learning framework emphasizing holistic education, research, innovation, and technology integration.

  • Holistic Education: Encourages multidisciplinary subject combinations.
  • Flexible Undergraduate System: Students can choose majors and minors.
  • Research & Innovation: Promotes critical thinking and creativity.
  • Technology Integration: Emphasizes digital tools and e-learning.
  • Skill Development: Focused on employability and industry collaboration.
  • Global Exposure: International partnerships and exchange opportunities.
