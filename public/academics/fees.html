Fees

  • Students should pay the prescribed fees on the online portal at the time of admission.
  • Fees will be accepted online through Debit Card / Credit Card / UPI / Net Banking / Mobile Banking.
  • Students are advised not to share payment details with unauthorized persons. The college will not be responsible for any loss.
  • Examination fees must be paid as per the dates prescribed by H.S.C. Board / University of Mumbai / College authorities.

Junior College

If a student informs the institution before commencement of the academic year about withdrawal, the institution shall refund tuition, term, and laboratory fees (if any), excluding admission fees.

Degree College

Candidates admitted to undergraduate courses may request a refund of fees by written application. The refund will be processed as per the following table:

Sr. No Period Deduction Charges
1Prior to commencement of academic term and instruction of the courseRs. 500/- lump sum
2Up to 20 days after commencement of academic term20% of total fees
3From 21st to 50th day after commencement30% of total fees
4From 51st to 80th day after commencement50% of total fees
5From 81st to 110th day after commencement60% of total fees
6After 111th day100% of total fees
  • Refund percentage is based on the total fees for the particular course.
  • Refunds will be made only by cheque/bank transfer within 30 working days.
  • Cheque/Bank transfer will be issued in the name of the student/parent only.
