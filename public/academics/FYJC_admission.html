FYJC (11th Std) Junior College Online Admission 2025–26

Online Admission Guidelines 2025–26:
Students must fill both the college admission form and the Department of Education, Govt. of Maharashtra forms (Application Form and Option Form).

Instructions for 11th Standard (FYJC) Admission Form Filling

  1. Students must register and login on the college portal and select one course by paying ₹50, then fill in personal, reservation, and educational details, upload documents, and submit the form.
  2. To apply for additional courses (e.g., Science IT or Bifocal Computer Science), pay ₹50 for each additional course.
  3. Mandatory document for online form filling is the computerized 10th Standard marksheet.
  4. Caste certificate (if applicable) should be scanned and attached.
  5. Students can apply under multiple categories (Minority/Inhouse/Management) by selecting the relevant options.

Important: Students applying under any quota must mention the name of R.J. College in the Option Form submitted on the DYDE portal.

Admission Process

Minority and Inhouse Admissions (Quota Admission)

  1. Online form sale and submission via College Portal: https://admission.rjcollege.edu.in/junior/#/login
  2. Merit lists will be displayed on the website and notifications will be issued as per the Education Department’s schedule.

Deputy Director (General/Open Admission)

  1. DYDE list will be displayed on the college website.
  2. Students from DYDE list must fill the online form on our portal to confirm admission.
  3. Admission must be completed within the stipulated time; otherwise, the seat will be forfeited.

Instructions to Students

Original documents to be submitted as and when demanded.

Documents to Upload (PDF format only)

For Students from Mumbai/Maharashtra Board

  • Original Leaving Certificate
  • Original Marksheet
  • Ration Card (for Girls Freeship – applicable only for first and second child)
  • DYDE Application Form

For Students from Other Boards (Eligibility Purpose)

  • Original Marksheet
  • Original Verified Transfer Certificate
  • Migration Certificate (verified by Zilla Parishad)
  • Ration Card for Girls Freeship
  • DYDE Application Form

Documents for Verification

  • Caste Certificate and Caste Validity Certificate (if applicable)
  • Ration Card showing girl child name (for girls freeship)
  • Aadhaar Card of student
  • Certificate for Physically Handicapped (if applicable)
  • Sports Certificates (if admission through sports quota)
  • Ex-Servicemen Certificate (if applicable)
  • Any other relevant document

Note:

  • Upload clear scanned copies of all documents; unclear uploads will lead to rejection.
  • Admission will be treated as provisional until verification of original documents.
  • Principal’s decision regarding admission is final and binding.

Courses Offered

The following FYJC streams and subjects are available:

  • FYJC Science (Aided) – English, Marathi/Hindi/Sanskrit, Maths, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, EVS, Health & Physical Education.
  • FYJC Science (IT - Self Finance) – English, Maths, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Information Technology, EVS, Health & Physical Education.
  • FYJC Bifocal Computer Science – English, Maths, Physics, Chemistry, Computer Science I & II, EVS, Health & Physical Education.
  • FYJC Commerce (Aided) – English, Second Language, Economics, Book Keeping, OCM, SP, Maths, EVS, Health & Physical Education.
  • FYJC Commerce (IT - Self Finance) – English, Economics, Book Keeping, OCM, Maths, Information Technology, EVS, Health & Physical Education.
  • FYJC Arts (Aided) – English, Second Language, History, Geography, Political Science, Sociology, Economics, EVS, Health & Physical Education.
