Anti-Ragging Policy

Ragging is totally banned in the college as per the UGC Regulations on Curbing the Menace of Ragging in Higher Educational Institutions, 2009. R.J. College strictly follows all the UGC norms and has established both an Anti-Ragging Committee and an Anti-Ragging Squad to ensure a safe, inclusive, and disciplined environment for all students.

UGC Regulations on Ragging

The UGC regulations were framed under Section 26 (1)(g) of the University Grants Commission Act, 1956, to prohibit and prevent any act of ragging in educational institutions. Every student is expected to comply with these norms and maintain a respectful academic environment.

What Constitutes Ragging?
  • Any act that teases, treats, or handles a student with rudeness or discomfort.
  • Indulging in rowdy or undisciplined activities causing psychological or physical harm.
  • Forcing a student to perform acts leading to shame or embarrassment.
  • Disrupting academic activities or coercing students into services.
  • Acts of physical, verbal, or sexual abuse including obscene acts or gestures.
  • Financial extortion or pressure for unnecessary expenditure.
  • Cyberbullying, public humiliation, or any form of online harassment.
  • Any behavior affecting a student’s mental health, confidence, or self-esteem.
