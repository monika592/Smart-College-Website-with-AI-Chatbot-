Placement Registration Portal (Powered by Pod.ai)

All students who wish to participate in campus placements must register through the official Placement Registration Portal. Please read the following instructions carefully before registration.

👉 First-time Registration: Click the button below to access the registration page:

Go to Placement Registration Portal

You will see the registration screen. Follow these steps carefully:

  1. Enter your First Name, Middle Name, and Last Name.
  2. Provide a valid active email address. A confirmation mail will be sent to this email ID.
  3. In the Enrollment No. field, enter your College Roll Number.
  4. In the Course Completion Year field — for example, if you are in TYBSc (2023–24), select 2024.
  5. Select your Course and click Sign Up.
  6. After submitting, you will receive a confirmation email with an activation link. Click it to activate your account.
  7. Once activated, log in to the portal using your registered email and password at https://rjcollege.pod.ai/.
  8. After logging in, complete your profile — fill in your personal details, academic records, and upload your latest resume (PDF).
  9. You will then be able to view all upcoming placement news and register for company drives through this portal.

⚠️ Last date for Registration: 15 October 2023

For technical or login issues, contact: placement@rjcollege.edu.in
