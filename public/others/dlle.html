Lifelong Learning and Extension (DLLE) Unit

The Lifelong Learning and Extension (DLLE) Unit at R.J. College is run in collaboration with DLLE, University of Mumbai. Started in 2007-08 under the leadership of Dr. Usha Mukundan, it promotes community welfare through diverse projects like Status of Women in Society, Career Project, National Institute of Open Schooling (NIOS), Industry Orientation Project (IOP), Annapurna Yojana, and Population Education Club (PEC). Undergraduate and postgraduate students actively participate under Extension Work Teachers, gaining soft skills with every activity. Awareness about social issues and personal development is fostered through guest lectures, community visits, and field programs.
DLLE-RJC Convenor: Dr. Vaishali Raikwar
