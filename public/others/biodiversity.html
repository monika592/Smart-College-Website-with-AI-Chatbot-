Biodiversity of RJ Campus

Introduction

Biodiversity is the existence of a number of different kinds of animals and plants which together make a good and healthy environment. Biodiversity survey is the study of biological diversity of a particular area, in which species of plant, animals and insects are enlisted.

Area of Survey

Campus area of Ramniranjan Jhunjhunwala College, situated in Ghatkopar (West), is studied for biodiversity. The college campus has a garden, a terrace garden, a green wall, aquaponics and hydroponics plants, cactus and succulent section, and a terrarium section.
