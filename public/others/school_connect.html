SCHOOL CONNECT 2.0

‘School Connect 2.0’ is a forward-thinking initiative aimed at creating a strong bridge between schools, higher education institutions, industries, and research organizations, ensuring that students are well-prepared to meet future challenges and opportunities. This program is designed to foster collaboration, promote career awareness, and provide practical exposure to help students transition smoothly from classroom learning to real-world applications.

A key component of School Connect 2.0 is creating awareness about the National Education Policy (NEP 2020) among school leaders, teachers, and students. The initiative highlights the transformative reforms proposed in NEP, focusing on skill development, experiential learning, multidisciplinary education, and holistic student development. By incorporating NEP guidelines, the program ensures that schools are aligned with the latest educational framework, promoting flexibility, innovation, and lifelong learning.

Through interactive sessions, workshops, industry visits, and expert talks, School Connect 2.0 offers a platform for schools to understand industry expectations, emerging careers, and the evolving role of education. The initiative also encourages schools to adopt innovative teaching practices and learner-centric approaches, in line with NEP’s vision.

Let’s work together to empower the next generation with the knowledge, skills, and values needed to thrive in a rapidly changing world!
