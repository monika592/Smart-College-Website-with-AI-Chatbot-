Prevention of Caste-Based Discrimination

Any kind of discrimination on the basis of caste, creed, religion, language, ethnicity, gender, and disability is strictly prohibited in R J College. The college is committed to maintaining an inclusive environment where all individuals are treated with respect and dignity.

RJC will be highly sensitive while dealing with incidents of caste discrimination, and strict action will be taken against such cases. The officials and faculty members are expected to refrain from any discriminatory behavior towards SC/ST students or any other community.

In the event of any such incident, RJC ensures that appropriate action is initiated promptly against the erring individuals. The college maintains transparency and fairness in handling grievances related to caste discrimination.

Students may lodge complaints about caste-based discrimination through the dedicated complaint register placed in the Registrar/Principal Office, or via the online form. Action Taken Reports will also be forwarded to the University Grants Commission and University of Mumbai when applicable.
