About Us

Hindi Vidya Prachar Samiti’s Ramniranjan Jhunjhunwala College of Arts, Science and Commerce with an Empowered Autonomous Status was established in 1963 by a visionary Postman Shri Nandkishore Singh Jairamji to provide quality education to the youth. Over six decades, the college has scaled several milestones in keeping with the rapidly changing educational and social needs of society.

Ramniranjan Jhunjhunwala College began as a conventional higher education institution but soon realized the need to prepare students with professional education and employability skills required by industries. In the last two decades, the college has introduced several programs to provide students with greater opportunities to excel in their chosen fields.

The college offers undergraduate and postgraduate courses in various disciplines including Science, Commerce, Arts, IT, Biotechnology, Management Studies, Data Science, Animation, and more. Academic restructuring includes courses aligned with NEP 2020 to enhance research, innovation, and interdisciplinary learning.

With certifications in ISO 9001, ISO 14001, and ISO 27001, the college ensures quality processes and is consistently accredited with ‘A’ Grade by NAAC. R.J. College stands as one of the most reputed autonomous colleges in Mumbai, promoting holistic development and stakeholder satisfaction in all endeavors.
