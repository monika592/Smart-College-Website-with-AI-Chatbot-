About the Principal



Dr. Himanshu Govindji Dawda Principal

It is my pleasure to welcome you to R.J. College of Arts, Science & Commerce (Empowered Autonomous). Our institution strives for academic excellence and holistic development of each student. Under the National Education Policy (NEP 2020) and our strong traditions, we aim to nurture future ready citizens with values, innovation and leadership.

In recent years, R.J. College has expanded its disciplinary offerings, strengthened research infrastructure, and provided dynamic student-centric programs including Data Science, Animation, VFX, Fashion Design, and more. We are committed to empowering the youth of Mumbai and beyond with opportunities to excel in a changing world.

We invite you to explore our departments, engage with our vibrant campus life, and join our journey toward excellence. Your success is our mission.
