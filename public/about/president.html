Profile of Dr. Rajendra Singh (President, HVPS)

Dr. Rajendra Singh

President, Hindi Vidya Prachar Samiti
A visionary educationist and successful businessman

Dr. Rajendra Singh was born on 25th January 1954, a resident of Verona, Hiranandani Garden Powai, Partner and Director in Suchita and Pashmina group of Real Estate Companies. His success story and contribution to empowering youth by providing education to the marginalized section is nothing short of inspiring.

He was a brilliant student who studied in Hindi High School, Ghatkopar in Hindi Medium and was a school topper. His journey proves that your mother tongue can never be a barrier to success. After completing his education, he contributed immensely to the Hindi Vidya Prachar Samiti, helping expand the reach and quality of education.

As a trustee and honorary secretary of Hindi Vidya Prachar Samiti, he played a pivotal role in getting Ramniranjan Jhunjhunwala College accredited by the NAAC. His leadership transformed the college into one of the leading educational institutions in Mumbai.

The college today caters to more than ten thousand students across various undergraduate and postgraduate programs, with an emphasis on innovation, research, and holistic learning. Dr. Rajendra Singh’s vision has been instrumental in achieving Autonomous Status for the college in 2018.
